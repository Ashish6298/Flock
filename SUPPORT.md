# Flock Support Guide

Welcome to the Flock Support Hub! Here are the channels to get assistance:

## Community Support

- **GitHub Discussions**: Best for architecture questions, best practices discussions, and sharing configuration setups.
- **GitHub Issues**: Best for reporting bugs, requesting features, and tracking release tasks.

## Enterprise Support

For production issues, SLA guarantees, custom GRC policies integration, and hybrid federation planning, please contact our enterprise solutions team.
